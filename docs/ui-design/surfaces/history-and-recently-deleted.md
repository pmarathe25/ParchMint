# History and Recently Deleted

- History uses list/detail with immediate read-only, side-by-side
  checkpoint/current comparison and word-level change highlights. Restore names
  the checkpoint and whole-project impact. Rows have no leading icons, and the
  design has no separate Preview or Compare buttons. Persisted date/session
  headings organize real checkpoints; checkpoints from older projects without a
  timestamp remain visibly undated rather than receiving invented dates.
  Recently Deleted shows one formatted preview and its restore location, never
  a diff.
